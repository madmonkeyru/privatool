Step by step

Reading an operator. Each digit is the original position of the symbol that ends up in that slot. 4 2 3 1 puts the 4th symbol first, then the 2nd, the 3rd and the 1st.

Given operators are already shown on the game screen, and the tool applies them first. Choose rows are the ones you pick from. Type the options left to right as shown. With more than one Choose row, enter the options for every row so the answer is unique.
